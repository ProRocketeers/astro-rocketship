# ProRocketeers - IT Services Company Summary

## Overview

**ProRocketeers** is a Czech IT company (based in Ostrava) that provides custom software development services. Their motto is "Your IT on Rocket Fuel."

## Services Offered

### 1. Team as a Service
- Builds custom 3-8 member teams for each client
- Team members cover all competencies needed for specific projects
- Uses agile approach with rapid delivery of functional units
- Known for proposing technical and process innovations
- Prefers long-term cooperation

**Process includes:**
- Requirements gathering
- Solution design
- Complexity estimation
- Architecture design
- Implementation
- Testing
- Deployment
- Optimization

### 2. Training and Consulting
- Project consultation and roadmapping
- Innovative solutions for complex projects
- Professional training in modern technologies and best practices
- Student academy for beginners (best interns can get permanent positions)

**Training topics:**
- Development
- Architecture
- Innovations
- Infrastructure
- Agile

### 3. Startup Studio
- Develops own products using innovative technologies
- Applies rapid development, prototyping, and customer feedback during wireframing
- Validates functionality via MVP
- Has experience from New York and Silicon Valley
- Offers startup development services including:
  - Angel investment
  - Development
  - Project management
  - Business representation
